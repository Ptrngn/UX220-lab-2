Aphantasia

A peace in my mind

I have always been a fan psychology and I recently read an arcticle about aphantasia. Aphantasia is a rare condition, it is the inability to voluntarity form "an eye in the mind", people who have it cannot imagine or picture things in their head.

Aphantasia can have both good and bad sides. On the positive side, people with aphantasia may be less affected by disturbing mental images and may focus more on facts and reality. However, it can make it difficult to visualize people, places, memories, or future ideas in their mind. Overall, aphantasia is not necessarily a disadvantage; it is simply a different way of experiencing and processing information.
